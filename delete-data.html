Delete your data

Last updated: 2 November 2026

Maple Falls: Murder Mystery has no accounts and no login. Your progress is stored on your device. Google Firebase keeps analytics and crash data linked to a random app-instance ID, not to your name or email.

Step 1: delete the data on your device

  1. Open the game and go to Settings → Delete my data, then confirm. This erases your progress, currency, choices and settings and starts the game fresh.
  2. Or uninstall the game, or go to Android Settings → Apps → Maple Falls → Storage → Clear storage.

Step 2: ask us to delete analytics and crash data

  1. Email superprogrammersaurabh@gmail.com with the subject "Maple Falls data deletion".
  2. So we can find your data, include the date you last played and your device model.
  3. We delete the Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics data linked to your app instance within 30 days and reply to confirm.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: game progress and settings on your device (step 1); Firebase Analytics events and user properties and Crashlytics reports for your app instance (step 2); your push-notification registration (turning notifications off in the game also removes it).
  • Kept by Google, not by us: purchase records (Google Play keeps them for payments, tax and refunds) and advertising data (manage it in Android Settings → Google → Ads, or at myaccount.google.com).
  • Without a request, Firebase Analytics data is deleted automatically after 14 months and Crashlytics reports after 90 days.
